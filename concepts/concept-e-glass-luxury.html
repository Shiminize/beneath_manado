Chapter 4

The house kept the morning at nineteen degrees. Not cool. Not warm. Correct.

Eline crossed the foyer by the marble seams, heel to edge, edge to heel, never cutting through the white center of a tile. The stone had been polished until it held the light without reflecting it.

Her shoes should have clicked. They did not.

The foyer took each step from her and stored it somewhere deep in the walls. There were no soft places here.
