Chapter 1: The Vacuum
13 pages left

The house kept the morning at nineteen degrees.

Not cool. Not warm. Correct.

Eline crossed the foyer by the marble seams, heel to edge, edge to heel, never cutting through the white center of a tile. The stone had been polished until it held the light without reflecting it.

Her shoes should have clicked.

They did not.

The foyer took each step from her and stored it somewhere deep in the walls.

Design Rationale

Nordic Fog strips the UI to essentials. Cool gray backgrounds (#f0f1f3) with pure white panels create a layered paper metaphor. Steel-blue (#4a6fa5) accents feel trustworthy without warmth; a secondary sage (#6b8f71) provides status differentiation. Outfit handles UI type with geometric clarity; Source Serif 4 gives the reader a refined editorial rhythm. Borders replace shadows wherever possible — the result is an interface that disappears during reading.
