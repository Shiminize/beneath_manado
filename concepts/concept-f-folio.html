Why this direction

Folio

Folio treats the app like a beautifully typeset book. It keeps the warm-paper soul the product already has, but removes every box, shadow, and saturated panel that competed with it. Hierarchy is built from type scale and hairline rules, not coloured cards. The one claret accent — drawn from The Friction of the Spark itself — appears only on progress, active states, and the drop-cap, so it always means "here, now."

  • Continue is the hero. The book you're 4% into anchors Home at full size; Top Picks drop to a quiet list beneath it.
  • One card system — none. Library and Top Picks become hairline-separated rows, so cover art is the only colour on the page.
  • Reader chrome collapses to two hairline bars. One progress line, one page label, two controls (Aa, contents). The text is the interface.
  • UI type grows up. Labels sit at 12–13px with editorial small-caps, replacing the sub-10px navigation text.
  • Desktop becomes a centred ~720px column with the same rules — no more stretched-mobile dead space.
