Chapter 1: The Vacuum
13 pages left

The house kept the morning at nineteen degrees.

Not cool. Not warm. Correct.

Eline crossed the foyer by the marble seams, heel to edge, edge to heel, never cutting through the white center of a tile. The stone had been polished until it held the light without reflecting it.

Her shoes should have clicked.

They did not.

The foyer took each step from her and stored it somewhere deep in the walls.

Design Rationale

Warm Parchment evolves the current oxide/sage direction into a cohesive warm palette. Cream backgrounds (#f5ede2) reference aged paper; terracotta (#b0623a) accents add warmth without the heaviness of the current teal. The continue card uses a dramatic gradient that matches the book's cover art tones. Literata serves as the reading font — designed specifically for long-form digital reading. DM Sans handles UI with friendly geometric shapes. Softer, larger border-radius values throughout create an organic, approachable feel.
