Chapter 1: The Vacuum
13 pages left in chapter

The house kept the morning at nineteen degrees.

Not cool. Not warm. Correct.

Eline crossed the foyer by the marble seams, heel to edge, edge to heel, never cutting through the white center of a tile. The stone had been polished until it held the light without reflecting it. There were no soft places here. No loose rugs. No crooked flowers.

Her shoes should have clicked.

They did not.

The foyer took each step from her and stored it somewhere deep in the walls.

Design Rationale

Midnight Library inverts the current sage-light default into a premium dark-first experience. The deep navy (#0f1218) provides true eye comfort for long evening reading, while warm gold (#c9a85c) accents give the UI an editorial, magazine-quality feel without competing with body text. Cormorant Garamond handles display type; Inter keeps controls crisp. Surfaces layer from app → panel → card → elevated with subtle 6-8 point lightness steps, creating depth without harshness.
