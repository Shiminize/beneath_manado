The house kept the morning at nineteen degrees.

Not cool. Not warm. Correct.

Eline crossed the foyer by the marble seams, heel to edge, edge to heel, never cutting through the white center of a tile. The stone had been polished until it held the light without reflecting it. There were no soft places here.

Her shoes should have clicked.

They did not.

Why this direction

Haven

Haven keeps the app warm and welcoming, but trades clutter for calm. Everything lives on one soft, rounded card system with gentle shadows and roomy padding — so the page feels organised and unhurried rather than busy. A single muted sage replaces the competing greens and reds; the only saturated colour left is the cover art itself.

  • Continue is a single hero card with a clear "Resume · 4%" action — the rest of Home arranges quietly beneath it.
  • One rounded card system everywhere. Top Picks and Library share the same soft surface, so nothing shouts louder than its neighbour.
  • The reader collapses to one floating dock — progress bar, page count, and page turns in a single pill. No scattered circles.
  • Sage tints carry state. Tags, active filters, and the goal ring all use one accent family at readable 12px+.
  • Desktop centres the cards in a calm column with the goal and Continue side-by-side, filling the canvas intentionally.
